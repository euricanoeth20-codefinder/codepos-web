CodePOS & CodeFinder respects all merchant and consumer data. Personal data collected during transactions (e.g. mobile phone numbers for WhatsApp slips) is processed solely for delivering digital tax invoices and is never shared, marketed, or sold to third parties.

Data is stored in isolated PostgreSQL multi-tenant databases with Row-Level Security (RLS) hosted in secure cloud nodes.

All subscription licenses operate on a month-to-month PayFast South Africa recurring billing protocol. Subscribers can cancel at any time with no lock-in contracts.

First-time account registrations are submitted into the Master Admin queue and verified directly by Eurica Noeth prior to live store till activation.

In accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA):

  • Purpose Limitation: Mobile numbers entered at checkout are solely for electronic slips.
  • Data Security: Transactions and till records are protected with 256-bit encryption.
  • Information Officer: Supervised under Eurica Noeth (codefindersa@gmail.com).

Staff PIN Login: Cashiers enter their 4-digit PIN to unlock the till. Only authorized departments are visible.

Scanning Modes: Cashiers can tap to type product names, point smartphone camera at barcodes, or take a photo for AI SKU identification.

Digital Slips: Enter the customer's phone number to send an instant slip over WhatsApp without thermal paper rolls.

5 SIMPLE STEPS TO RING UP SALES IN UNDER 5 MINUTES:

• STEP 1: Save CodePOS To Your Phone Home Screen (Zero App Store Downloads): - On iPhone/iPad: Open Safari > go to www.codepos.co.za > Tap "Share" icon (square with upward arrow) > Tap "Add to Home Screen".
- On Android: Open Chrome > go to www.codepos.co.za > Tap three dots (⋮) top-right > Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App".
- On Windows Laptop: Open Chrome/Edge > click computer screen icon on URL bar ("Install CodePOS").
• STEP 2: First-Time Sign-In & Staff PIN Setup: - Tap your new CodePOS home screen icon.
- Tap "Client & Store Terminal Sign-In" and enter your Store Email & Master PIN received via welcome WhatsApp.
- Go to "Staff PIN Manager" to add 4-digit PINs for cashiers/waiters (e.g., 1001, 1002). Cashiers cannot see financial turnover!
• STEP 3: Select Your Operating Venue Layout: - Restaurant Floor Plan: Squircle table buttons (Table 1, Table 2). Tap table to fire drinks and meals to kitchen pass.
- Guesthouse & Lodge Folio: Allocate honesty bar or room service directly to room accounts.
- Cafe Terrace & Spaza Queue: Fast counter order grids with instant barcode scans.
• STEP 4: Ringing Up A Sale & Sending Digital WhatsApp Slips: - Tap items to add to cart.
- Tap "CHECKOUT / TENDER": Cash calculates change automatically. Card swipe verified on your speedpoint.
- Enter customer's cell (e.g. 082 123 4567) and tap "SEND WHATSAPP SLIP". Instant legal South African 15% VAT tax invoice! Zero paper rolls to buy.
• STEP 5: End-of-Day Cash-Up & Daily Z-Report: - Tap "Daily Cash-Up". Count physical cash in till and enter total.
- Tap "Reconcile Shift": CodePOS shows exact variance and creates your official Daily Z-Report archive in 1 tap.

ELIMINATE LONG TILL QUEUES & CASHIER BARCODE ERRORS:

• STEP 1: Staff Phone Viewfinder Access: Open CodeFinder and sign in with the 4-digit staff PIN. The frontline camera viewfinder opens immediately.
• STEP 2: The 3 Search Modes (Use Whichever is Fastest): - Mode 1 (Text & Categories): Type first few letters (e.g. "Cro" for Croissant). Tap category button (Bakery, Butchery, Produce).
- Mode 2 (Phone Camera Barcode): Tap "Barcode Mode". Point rear camera at retail barcode (EAN-13, UPC). Beeps in under 200ms.
- Mode 3 (Google Vertex AI Photo Match): Tap "AI Camera Mode". Point camera at unpackaged bakery pastry or fresh produce. Neural vision matches texture & shape in 600ms with 95%+ confidence.
• STEP 3: Bluetooth Shelf-Edge Label Printing: Looking at any product in CodeFinder, tap "Print Shelf Label". Connects to portable 58mm/80mm Bluetooth belt printer in under 2 seconds. Spools out adhesive thermal price label in ZAR!
• STEP 4: Recipe Batch Costing & Gross Profit % Meter: In Owner portal, enter ingredient costs and portion yield. CodeFinder calculates portion cost and displays your live GP% dial.

• STEP 1: Setting Up Staff Bench & Stations: Log in on laptop or tablet. Tap "Staff Bench" to add employees with role tags (Cashier, Supervisor, Waiter, Chef), hourly rates, and cell numbers. Confirm daily shift times.
• STEP 2: The 7-Day Interactive Matrix Grid: Drag and drop staff from left bench into Monday-to-Sunday slots. Live ZAR wage counter tallies projected weekly payroll in real time at the top.
• STEP 3: 1-Click AI Auto-Scheduler: Tap "AI Smart Auto-Schedule". The algorithm factors availability and mandatory rest periods to populate balanced shift rotations in 3 seconds!
• STEP 4: WhatsApp Shift Broadcast (Zero App Downloads): Tap "Publish & Broadcast Roster". Code SmartRoster compiles personalized schedule messages delivered directly to each employee's personal WhatsApp.
• STEP 5: BCEA Labor Law Safeguards: Automatically flags if an employee exceeds 45 ordinary weekly hours or has less than 36 consecutive hours of statutory rest.

Select an operational manual below for step-by-step setup and layman operating procedures:

Manual 1: CodePOS™ (Zero-Hardware Mobile POS) Open

Step 1 (Add to Home Screen): Open Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) at codepos.co.za. Tap 'Share' > 'Add to Home Screen'. Runs offline during loadshedding.

Step 2 (Ringing Sales): Tap table or item, tap Tender (Cash or Speedpoint Card). For WhatsApp slips, type client cell and tap Send Slip.

Step 3 (Daily Cash-Up): Tap 'Cash-Up' at shift end, count cash in drawer, generate instant Z-Report.

Manual 2: CodeFinder™ (AI Camera SKU Search) Open

3 Search Modes: 1) Type item name. 2) Point camera at retail barcode. 3) Snap photo of unpackaged baked goods or fresh produce for 600ms AI match.

Shelf Labels: Tap 'Print Label' to spool price tags over Bluetooth to portable belt printers.

Manual 3: Code SmartRoster™ (7-Day Shift Matrix) Open

Drag & Drop: Pull staff names from the bench into morning, day, or night shifts. AI auto-schedules in 1 click.

WhatsApp Broadcast: Tap 'Publish' to dispatch personalized weekly rosters directly to employees' phones.

Manual 4: Code Time Management System™ (Biometric Clock-In) Open

Counter Tablet Setup: Mount any standard tablet at staff entrance in full screen mode.

Clock-In (2 Seconds): Staff enter 4-digit PIN. Front camera takes 200ms facial verification to stop buddy punching.

Payroll Export: 1-click CSV export ready for Sage Pastel, VIP, and Xero.

POPIA Act Privacy Framework

In accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA No. 4 of 2013), CodePOS™ and CodeFinder™ collect only necessary operational data.

Customer mobile numbers and email addresses inputted at checkout are encrypted and utilized exclusively for instant digital receipt delivery via WhatsApp or email. Customer information is never sold or shared.

All merchant transactional records are isolated via tenant Row-Level Security (RLS) in South African data centres and maintained for statutory compliance.

Terms of Use & PayFast Subscription

CodePOS™ and CodeFinder™ operate on monthly software subscriptions billed in South African Rand (ZAR) via PayFast South Africa. Subscriptions may be cancelled at any time.

Store owners retain 100% ownership of their product databases, pricing lists, and recipe ingredient costing ledgers.

Software is licensed for commercial point-of-sale and barcode search operations on compatible Android, iOS, and Windows devices.
